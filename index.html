Naghmeh Farzi

PhD Candidate in Computer Science · University of New Hampshire

I am a Computer Science PhD candidate at the University of New Hampshire, advised by Prof. Laura Dietz. My research sits at the intersection of Information Retrieval and AI evaluation. I build transparent, rubric-based frameworks that use LLMs as judges to assess and improve retrieval and RAG systems. I am also interested in model behavior and architecture, as well as the intersection of AI and social sciences.
